My Armory

private gun inventory

A private inventory and recordkeeping app for firearms, ammunition, optics, and gear. Everything is stored in an encrypted database on your device — no accounts, no servers, no analytics. We couldn't see your collection if we wanted to.

Organize

Six item types with the fields collectors actually use, custom fields, storage locations with visual shelf layouts, collections, encrypted photos, and a document vault for receipts and tax stamps.

Track

Round counters with automatic ammo draw-down and low-stock alerts, maintenance logs with cleaning reminders, range trips, named equipment setups, and a value & usage dashboard.

Protect

Optional passphrase protection with real encryption and no recovery backdoor, biometric unlock, automatic locking, and encrypted backups that only ever go where you send them.
