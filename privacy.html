My Armory Privacy Policy

This policy also ships, in full, inside the app (Settings ▸ About).

The short version: your data never leaves your device unless you explicitly send it somewhere, and we couldn't read it if it did.

What we collect

Nothing. My Armory has no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no advertising, and no crash reporting. The developer receives no data of any kind from the app.

Where your data lives

Everything you enter — items, photos, documents, logs — is stored in an encrypted database on your device. If you enable passphrase protection, the encryption key is derived from your passphrase and cannot be recovered by anyone, including us.

Network access

My Armory has no network code of its own and no servers to talk to: nothing you enter is ever transmitted, and the developer receives nothing. The Google Play Billing library that sells the Pro upgrade brings along a Google component which declares the INTERNET permission; that belongs to Google Play, not to us, and it carries none of your collection data. (The non-Play build of the app requests no INTERNET permission at all.) When you back up, the encrypted file is written on your device and you choose what happens next — save it, or send it to Drive, email, or your computer through Android's share sheet. In that case the app you choose does the sending, under your own account, and the file is already encrypted before it leaves.

Exports and sharing

PDF and CSV exports, backups, and transfer sheets are created on-device and go only where you choose to send them via Android's share sheet. What each export includes is shown before it is created, and sensitive fields can be excluded.

OS backups

Android's automatic cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are disabled for this app so the operating system never copies your data anywhere.

Permissions

Camera — only for scanning barcodes on ammunition boxes; frames are processed on-device and never stored or transmitted. Notifications — only for reminders you create. Biometrics — only to unlock the app on your device.

Children

My Armory is intended for adults and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version ships inside the app and at this URL, with the change noted in release notes.
